AI-Powered Innovations in Nuclear Medicine

A summary of projects driving the future of diagnostics and clinical efficiency.

Enhancing Diagnostic Imaging

Synthetic CT (sCT) Generation

Usefulness: By generating high-quality anatomical CT scans from functional PET scans, this project provides vital context that helps clinicians more accurately locate tumors and understand disease progression. It improves diagnostic confidence and enables more precise treatment planning without additional radiation exposure.

Evolutionary 3D Geometric Modeling

Usefulness: This project creates self-improving AI that generates perfect 3D models of medical images ("supervoxels"). This is crucial for accurately measuring the size and shape of tumors or organs, leading to better-quantified disease assessment and more reliable clinical research.

Streamlining Clinical Workflows

Semi-Automatic Segmentation Pipeline

Usefulness: This tool dramatically accelerates the tedious process of outlining organs and tumors in medical scans. By providing an AI-generated first draft for clinicians to review, it speeds up research and ensures that annotations are consistent and reproducible, which is critical for clinical trials.

Clinical Decision Support for Thyroid Cancer

Usefulness: This system acts as an expert "second opinion" for doctors. It translates complex medical guidelines into clear, patient-specific recommendations for treating thyroid cancer. This helps standardize care, reduce errors, and ensure that every patient benefits from the latest evidence-based practices.

Unlocking Medical Data

German Radiology Report Analyzer

Usefulness: This project unlocks the valuable information trapped in unstructured, text-based radiology reports. By converting them into structured, searchable data, it enables large-scale research, helps identify trends in disease, and makes clinical data more Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR).
