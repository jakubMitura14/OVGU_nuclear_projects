Synthetic CT (sCT) Generation: Adding Anatomy to Function

This project addresses a fundamental challenge in nuclear medicine: PET scans show metabolic activity but lack anatomical detail. Our ensemble of AI "experts" generates a highly-accurate synthetic CT scan directly from the PET data.

Clinical Impact:

  • Enhanced Diagnostic Confidence: Provides crucial anatomical context to precisely locate metabolic hotspots.
  • Improved Attenuation Correction: Leads to more accurate SUV quantification in PET imaging.
  • More Precise Treatment Planning: Enables clinicians to better define the boundaries of tumors for radiotherapy.
  • Reduced Radiation Dose: Avoids the need for a separate, full-dose CT scan, minimizing patient exposure.
